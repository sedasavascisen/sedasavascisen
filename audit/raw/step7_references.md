## [28] ve [38] atıf desteği

- **[28] s. 2 of 19** — “Among recent hybrids, Nguyen, Hoang, and Cheema [28] combined CNN and LSTM for RIS-NOMA channel estimation without addressing phase wrapping.” → **destekliyor**. Abstract (OpenAlex/IEEE, CC BY): "This paper proposes a deep learning (DL)-based channel estimation method using a convolutional long-short term memory (CNN-LSTM) model for RIS-NOMA wireless communication systems". Full text (Ulster repository) contains no discussion of phase wrapping; phases are fixed: "we fix phase shift by generating fixed phase shifts in [0.01π, 0.02π]". (https://pure.ulster.ac.uk/ws/files/120376337/Channel_Estimation_Using_CNN_LSTM_in_RIS_NOMA_Assisted_6G_Network.pdf)
- **[28] s. 8 of 19** — “LSTM recurrence thus captures structured inter-element spatial dependencies—analogous to PixelRNN for spatially ordered pixels [28].” → **desteklemiyor**. Full text contains no mention of PixelRNN or pixels; in [28] the LSTM models time, not element order: "CNN can capture special features while LSTM can capture temporal features of time-series data" and "the features from CNN are fed into LSTM for decoding, and in turn extract the temporal correlation". (https://pure.ulster.ac.uk/ws/files/120376337/Channel_Estimation_Using_CNN_LSTM_in_RIS_NOMA_Assisted_6G_Network.pdf)
- **[38] s. 2 of 19** — “Tang et al. [38] characterized the performance of discrete phase shifters across bit-widths from 1-bit to continuous resolution, showing that 3-bit quantization (8 discrete levels) incurs below 10% performance loss relative to continuous control while substantially simplifying the hardware.” → **desteklemiyor**. [38] is a path-loss modeling + measurement paper. Abstract: "we refine our previously proposed free-space path loss model for RISs ... Two fabricated RISs operating in the millimeter-wave (mmWave) band are utilized to carry out a measurement campaign." The only bit-width in the full text (arXiv:2101.08607v2) is 1-bit: "Both metasurfaces are phase-programmable with 1-bit coding." No bit-width sweep, no 3-bit result, no '10%' figure. (https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.08607)
- **[38] s. 14 of 19** — “1-bit → 4.12°, 2-bit → 3.08°, 3-bit → 2.81° RMSE (continuous: 2.77°). Two-bit quantization recovers near-continuous performance, consistent with [38].” → **desteklemiyor**. Same as above: [38] reports only 1-bit prototypes ("Both metasurfaces are phase-programmable with 1-bit coding.") and makes no 2-bit vs continuous comparison. (https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.08607)
