\section{System and Channel Model}
\label{sec:system}

\subsection{Signal Model}
A BS with a uniform linear array of $N_t=8$ half-wavelength-spaced antennas serves $K=4$ single-antenna users through a direct link and an RIS of $N=32$ passive elements (an $8\times 4$ planar array); Section~\ref{sec:res_scaling} also uses $N=64$. With phases $\bs{\theta}\in[0,2\pi)^N$, $\mbf{v}=e^{j\bs{\theta}}$ and precoder $\mbf{W}=[\mbf{w}_1,\dots,\mbf{w}_K]$, user $k$ receives
\begin{equation}
    y_k = \left(\mbf{h}_{d,k}^H + \mbf{g}_k^H \operatorname{diag}(\mbf{v})\mbf{F}\right)\sum_{j=1}^K \mbf{w}_j s_j + n_k ,
    \label{eq:signal}
\end{equation}
where $\mbf{F}\in\mathbb{C}^{N\times N_t}$ is the BS--RIS channel, $\mbf{g}_k^H$ the RIS--user channel, $\mbf{h}_{d,k}^H$ the direct channel and $n_k\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,\sigma^2)$. With $\mbf{h}_k^H(\bs{\theta})$ the bracketed effective channel, user $k$ has rate $R_k=\log_2(1+\mathrm{SINR}_k)$, $\mathrm{SINR}_k = |\mbf{h}_k^H \mbf{w}_k|^2/(\sum_{j\ne k}|\mbf{h}_k^H \mbf{w}_j|^2 + \sigma^2)$. The precoder is regularized zero forcing~\cite{Peel2005TCOM} on the effective channel, with regularizer $\lambda = K\sigma^2/P$ and total power $P$. The objective is the minimum user rate, a fairness criterion also used for RIS-assisted MISO in~\cite{Nadeem2020TWC},
\begin{equation}
    \max_{\bs{\theta}} \; \min_k R_k(\bs{\theta}),
    \label{eq:maxmin}
\end{equation}
which Adam and the learned update optimize through the smooth surrogate $-\tfrac{1}{\beta}\log\sum_k e^{-\beta R_k}$, $\beta=8$; coordinate descent and the long-run Adam reference maximize the hard minimum.

The room is $40\times 20\times 6$~m (Fig.~\ref{fig:scenario}). The RIS is centred at $(20, 0.1, 3)$~m on a wall; the BS is at $(20, 6, 4)$~m, in the panel's boresight and beyond its Fraunhofer distance. Users are dropped uniformly at heights of 1--2~m. The carrier is $f_c=3$~GHz, the bandwidth 20~MHz, the transmit power 20~dBm, the BS antenna gain 5~dBi and the noise figure 7~dB. All links follow the 3GPP TR~38.901 indoor-office model~\cite{3gpp_channel}: its path loss, LoS probability, Ricean factor and log-normal shadowing with Gudmundson correlation~\cite{Gudmundson1991} at 5~m. Non-LoS components come from point scatterers shared by all links of a scenario. The cascaded amplitude follows the two-hop model of~\cite{Tang2021TWC} with a $\cos^q$ element pattern. Because an RIS is deployed where the direct path is obstructed, the direct link carries an extra 40~dB of loss.

\subsection{Mobility}
Users walk in straight lines at $v=1.5$~m/s (5.4~km/h) and are reflected at the walls. This lies between the 3~km/h of the 3GPP indoor-hotspot evaluation~\cite{3gpp_tr38913} and the 10~km/h upper end of the IMT-2020 pedestrian class, at which indoor mobility is evaluated~\cite{itu_m2410}; Section~\ref{sec:res_sensitivity} repeats the main measurements at these and higher speeds. Scatterers, path gains, LoS states, shadowing and $\mbf{F}$ are held fixed; only the users move, and $\mbf{g}_k(t)$ and $\mbf{h}_{d,k}(t)$ are recomputed from geometry at every instant. The reference time scale is the Doppler frequency $f_d=vf_c/c=15$~Hz, with coherence time $T_c=0.423/f_d=28.2$~ms~\cite{Clarke1968BSTJ,rappaport_wireless}.

\emph{Limitations.} Fixed scatterers make the channel change less than a real one over seconds, so effective rates beyond 200~ms are optimistic for slow methods. Scattered-path gains are drawn independently per BS antenna, which makes all rates optimistic. Channel knowledge is perfect apart from the acquisition delay we add, and the model is narrowband and far-field.
