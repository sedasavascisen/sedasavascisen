\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\emph{RIS phase optimization.} Joint precoding and phase design has been addressed with alternating optimization and semidefinite relaxation~\cite{Wu2019TWC}, practical phase-dependent amplitude models~\cite{Abeywickrama2020TCOM}, block coordinate descent~\cite{Pan2020TWC}, fractional programming~\cite{Guo2020TWC}, fixed-point and manifold methods~\cite{Yu2019ICCC} and weighted minimum mean-square error (WMMSE) methods~\cite{Choi2024TCOM} built on the algorithm of~\cite{Shi2011TSP}. These works report the objective value reached; run time, when discussed, is a complexity order, not a latency measured against the coherence time.

\emph{Learning.} Learned policies include deep reinforcement learning for joint precoding and phase design~\cite{Huang2020JSAC} and learning to reflect directly from pilots~\cite{Jiang2021JSAC}. Algorithm unrolling turns an optimizer's iterations into network layers~\cite{Monga2021SPM}; it has been applied to RIS beamforming~\cite{Liu2022TWC}, MIMO detection~\cite{Samuel2019TSP} and WMMSE precoding~\cite{Hu2021TWC,Pellaco2022OJCOMS}, and trained networks can approximate WMMSE at a fraction of its run time~\cite{Sun2018TSP}. Phase-aware regression, which predicts the sine and cosine of each phase, removes the $\pm\pi$ discontinuity of direct phase regression~\cite{SavasciSen2026AISY}. Learned methods are usually motivated by speed, but that speed is rarely related to how fast the channel changes, and classical baselines are rarely tuned or timed under the same conditions.

\emph{Mobility and outdated channels.} A vehicle-mounted intelligent refracting surface, combined with a two-stage channel estimation and refraction design, converts the end-to-end channel from fast to slow fading~\cite{Huang2022TWC}. Channel aging~\cite{Papazafeiropoulos2023TVT} and outdated channel knowledge~\cite{Zhang2022TCOM} have been analyzed for RIS, predictive beamforming forecasts the position and beam direction of fast unmanned aerial vehicles~\cite{Liu2021WCL}, and active sensing tracks mobile users~\cite{Han2025TWC}. Aging is classical in massive MIMO~\cite{Truong2013JCN,Chopra2018TWC}, as is limited channel feedback in broadcast channels~\cite{Jindal2006IT}. Two-timescale designs update the phases from channel statistics and the precoder from instantaneous channels~\cite{Zhao2021TWC,Cao2022TWC}. Practical surfaces have a few bits of phase resolution~\cite{Wu2020TCOM,Zhao2024OJCOMS}; we use continuous phases. In all of these works the delay between channel acquisition and use, where it is modeled at all, is a fixed system parameter; the optimizer's own run time, which differs from method to method and from one iteration count to the next, is not part of the model.

\emph{Gap.} To our knowledge, no RIS work evaluates each method on the channel at its own measured completion time, shows that this creates an optimal iteration count, or compares learned and classical optimizers both at equal steps and at equal measured time.
