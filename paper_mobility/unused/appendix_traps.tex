Three errors produced wrong numbers during this work, and each led to a rule in Section~\ref{sec:method}. (i)~A timing of Adam with $L=8$ taken while a training job was running gave 29.70~ms, against 17.48~ms without it (project log), and put a learned configuration on the latency--rate front; timings are now taken with no other job running, the load average is recorded where the script allows it, and dominance is reported only if it holds in every round. (ii)~An interactively computed ratio of fresh to stale beam, ``17$\times$'', is 11.7--14.2$\times$ when measured with a script (Table~\ref{tab:beam}); every number now comes from a script. (iii)~A summary that had not been regenerated after a re-measurement listed older timings; tables are generated directly from the result files.
